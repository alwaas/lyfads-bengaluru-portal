Frequently Asked Questions

How quickly can campaigns go live?

For performance marketing & paid media, campaigns go live within 5-7 business days after access handover. Video productions take 10-14 days from script signoff to final color grade.

Do you work on performance retainers?

Yes, for qualifying scaleups and e-commerce brands spending ₹5L+/month, we offer hybrid retainer + rev-share models aligned directly with ROAS and revenue targets.

Can we visit the Bengaluru studio?

Absolutely! We host founders and CMOs regularly for live script reads, studio shoot supervision, and growth workshops in our Koramangala facility.
